Before your first session.

Why install an app?

The installed app handles system audio and its floating window directly on your computer. A website needs a browser sharing picker and cannot provide the same desktop capture experience.

What happens to my audio and résumé?

Your résumé is read on your computer. While enabled, audio streams to Deepgram for transcription. When you request an answer, your question, résumé, job description, and instructions go to the AI provider you choose. Keys are held in memory for the current app session.

Is this a subscription?

This preview uses your own provider API keys. CueCard does not collect a subscription payment. Your AI and transcription providers may charge for usage.

Can I use it on every call?

Use CueCard for training, preparation, and calls where assistance is permitted. Audio support depends on operating system permissions. We do not promise invisibility in screen sharing or concealment from monitoring tools.
